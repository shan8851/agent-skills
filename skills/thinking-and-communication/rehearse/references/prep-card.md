# Prep card template

```markdown
**Goal:** [the outcome you want] · **Would accept:** [minimum] · **Walk-away:** [point/alternative, if relevant]

**Opening line:** "[one or two sentences in your voice that set up the ask]"

**Key points**
1. [point]: [evidence/example]
2. [point]: [evidence/example]
3. [point]: [evidence/example]

**Expect them to say:**
- "[likely pushback 1]"
- "[likely pushback 2]"
- "[likely pushback 3]"

**Check before the real thing:** [facts to confirm, documents to bring]
```

Keep it to what fits on a phone screen. If a section is irrelevant (no walk-away for an apology), drop it.
