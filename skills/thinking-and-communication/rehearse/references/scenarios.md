# Common scenarios

Use these as prompts for setup and as a source of realistic pushback. Adapt to what the user tells you; don't force a scenario onto them.

## Pay rise / salary negotiation / job offer
- **Setup:** target number, minimum, walk-away or alternatives, evidence (achievements, scope changes, market data to verify), budget cycle timing.
- **Pushback:** "budgets are fixed", "you're at the top of the band", "let me take it to HR", "what number did you have in mind?", "we can't match that, but…", "this is our best offer".
- **Coaching notes:** anchor with a specific number backed by reasons, then be comfortable with silence. Get process and dates, not vague promises. Negotiate the whole package (title, review date, flexibility, development) if salary is capped.

## Giving difficult feedback (to a colleague, report or peer)
- **Setup:** the specific behaviour, its impact, what they want instead, and the relationship.
- **Pushback:** defensiveness ("that's not fair"), deflection ("everyone does it"), upset, minimising.
- **Coaching notes:** describe the behaviour and its impact, not character. Invite their view. Agree one concrete next step.

## Raising a problem with a manager
- **Setup:** the issue, evidence, what they want (support, change, decision), and the manager's likely priorities.
- **Pushback:** "is it really that bad?", "can you handle it yourself?", "now's not a good time".
- **Coaching notes:** lead with the impact on work or the team, bring one proposed solution, and ask for a follow-up date.

## Landlord / letting agent repair
- **Setup:** the problem, when it started, previous contact (dates), photos or evidence, the outcome wanted and a timeline.
- **Pushback:** "we'll get someone out" (with no date), "that's tenant damage", "it's not urgent", silence.
- **Coaching notes:** get a specific date, follow up in writing, keep a log. Don't state legal rights unless the user has checked them for their location. Suggest where to check (e.g. Shelter or Citizens Advice in the UK).

## Saying no / setting a boundary
- **Setup:** what's being asked, what they're willing to do instead (if anything), and how the relationship matters.
- **Pushback:** guilt ("I thought I could count on you"), pressure ("it's just this once"), negotiation ("what about half?").
- **Coaching notes:** a short, kind and clear no, without over-explaining. Offer an alternative only if they genuinely mean it.

## Difficult personal or family conversation
- **Setup:** what they want the other person to understand, what they need from them, and topics to avoid.
- **Pushback:** changing the subject, bringing up the past, emotional escalation, "you're overreacting".
- **Coaching notes:** use "I" statements, one topic at a time, and know when to pause the conversation. Keep tough mode realistic, not cruel. If the material suggests abuse or danger, stop the role-play and prioritise their safety and support.

## Complaint to a company
- **Setup:** what happened (with dates), evidence, the outcome wanted (refund, repair, apology), and previous contact.
- **Pushback:** scripted responses, "there's nothing I can do", transfers, partial offers.
- **Coaching notes:** be calm and specific, ask for a reference number, escalate politely ("who can authorise that?"), and confirm everything in writing.
