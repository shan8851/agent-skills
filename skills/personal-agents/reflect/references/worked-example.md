# Worked example: "how's my week been?"

Fictional user. Today is Sunday 11 October 2026. The ledger says the last reflection was 4 October.

## Notes in the window (5–11 October)

- `journal/2026-10-06.md`: "Passport finally arrived. One less thing."
- `journal/2026-10-07.md`: "Late night again, scrolling till 1am. Knackered at work."
- `journal/2026-10-08.md`: "Finished the first draft of the grant application!! Took ages but it's done. Need to send it to Maya for comments by Friday."
- `journal/2026-10-09.md`: "Slept badly. Phone in bed again."
- `inbox.md` (2026-10-10): "Maybe try a pottery class?"
- The helper also flags `projects/grant.md` as edited: a budget table was updated on 10 October.

Ledger before: "Renew passport" open; "Book boiler service" open, due 31 October, last raised 4 October; no sleep theme.

## Reconciling

- Passport: resolved (6 October note). Close it.
- Grant: "send to Maya by Friday" is an explicit commitment with a date (Friday 9 October). No note says it was sent. Status unknown, not "missed".
- Boiler: no new evidence; due in 20 days; raised last week. Don't raise again.
- Sleep: two notes, plus one on 2 October from last window. Enough to mention gently, once.
- Pottery: an idea. Not a task.

## Response

```markdown
Good week on the things that had been hanging around: the grant draft is done (journal, 8 Oct), which you'd described as taking ages, and the passport finally arrived (6 Oct).

Sleep came up a few times: late scrolling on the 7th and the phone in bed on the 9th, and a similar note on 2 October. That might be worth keeping an eye on, if it matters to you.

**Still open**
- Grant draft to Maya for comments — you'd planned to send it by Friday 9 Oct, and the notes don't say whether it went (journal, 8 Oct). Has it?

_Covered: 5–11 October, 6 notes read, including the edited grant budget._
```

## After the user replies "yes, sent it Thursday"

(The user's reply is an explicit update, so it's saved to their notes, not only to the ledger.)


- Capture saves a one-line update in `projects/grant.md` (2026-10-11): "Sent the draft to Maya on Thursday." Event date: 8 October 2026 (the Thursday before the capture date).
- Ledger: grant item resolved, source `projects/grant.md`. Sleep theme added, last raised 11 October. Passport moved to Closed.

## Why this is good

- It leads with progress, because there was some.
- One pattern, with three dated sources, framed as an observation.
- No list of advice; the pottery idea isn't turned into a task, and the boiler isn't re-nagged.
- The grant item is asked about, not declared missed.
- Coverage is stated in one line.
